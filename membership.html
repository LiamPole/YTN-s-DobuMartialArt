FAQ

Questions before you join?

Here are a few quick answers. If you need something more specific, contact the DoBu team.

Contact DoBu
Can beginners join?

Yes. Basic membership is designed as a simple starting point, and training can progress as your experience grows.

Can I train more than one martial art?

Advanced allows any 2 martial arts, while Elite provides unlimited classes.

Can I change my membership later?

Your plan can be reviewed as your training needs change. Contact the academy for the appropriate next step.

Do I need previous martial arts experience?

No previous experience is required to begin. Coaches can help you choose a suitable starting level.

Is private tuition available?

Yes. Private tuition is available at £15 per hour for focused individual coaching.
